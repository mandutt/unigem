## 2. Fit gate (signal 이전)

```text
fit_ratio = loss_last / loss_first
fit_pass = fit_ratio <= 0.35
```

- E01/E11 × 3 seeds **모두** fit_pass여야 한다.
- 하나라도 실패하면 `NOT_EVALUABLE_OPTIMIZATION`; signal을 읽지 않는다.
- 0.35은 F0.1a에서 사전 사용한 gate를 그대로 유지한다.

## 3. Signal endpoint

- final checkpoint, donor-crop
- 15 `common_ids` × 5 donors
- `delta_c(s,d) = mean_donor(CE_wrong − CE_real)`
- E01/E11 copy primary; full secondary
- cell CI: 3-seed doc mean, `bootstrap_paired_v2(n_boot=10000, seed=20261109)`
- primary `D(d) = delta_E01(d) − delta_E11(d)`, copy
- `SIGNAL(c,copy)`: mean ≥ .05, CI.lower > 0, 2/3 seeds > 0
- `SIGNAL(c,full)`: mean > 0, CI.lower > 0, 2/3 seeds > 0
- `PASS_POSITIVE`: mean_D > 0 AND CI_D.lower > .05 AND SIGNAL(E01,copy) AND 2/3 D_s > 0
- `EQUIVALENT`: 95% CI_D entirely within [−.05,+.05]
- `ALL_FAIL`: E01/E11 × copy/full all non-signal
- else `INDETERMINATE`
- `NOT_EVALUABLE` if fit gate or endpoint completeness fails
