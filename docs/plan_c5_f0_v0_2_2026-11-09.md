## 3. 정의

- EG2 layer19 h, 512 dims, token prefix 753 for M.
- Adapter: 512→512, Linear/GELU/Linear/LayerNorm, random init.
- Decoder LoRA: 64 mT5 q/k/v/o modules, r8 alpha16 dropout .05.
- Training: AdamW, wd .01, adapter lr 1e-3, LoRA lr 1e-4, 100 updates, batch 1, order train20[step%20], clip 1.0, base decoder eval.
- M: M_POS_MEAN/M_NORM, K=753, 15 common, 120 records/cell/seed.
- Signal: donor-crop K=min(len(real),len(donor)), 20 val docs, 5 donors.
- Tail: signal tail only for E01/E11.
- Thresholds/seed/donor/step/LR: frozen Phase 6 values; no changes.

## 5. Endpoints and decision

### Primary

`E01 − E11` copy delta, 15 common docs, seed-average before bootstrap:

```text
delta_E01(doc) - delta_E11(doc)
```

- 1,000 resamples, PCG64 seed 20261109.
- Stage R 3 seeds; no 1-seed gate.
- Signal predicates use frozen Phase 6 definitions:
  - copy mean ≥ .05, CI lower >0, 2/3 seeds >0
  - full mean >0, CI lower >0, 2/3 seeds >0

### Interpretation (pre-registered)

- **E01 positive AND E11 negative AND E01−E11 >0**: adapter-associated signal loss supported; H3/LN/projection/residual is the next diagnostic.
- **E01 negative AND E11 negative**: this configured EG2-raw→mT5-decoder route does not show donor-specific signal; **not** proof that EG2 features lack information. Track decision separate.
- **E01 positive AND E11 positive**: adapter not necessary under this regime; no structural adapter claim.
- **E01 ≈ E11**: adapter effect not supported.
- No positive result uniquely proves adapter architecture; no negative result proves feature insufficiency.

## 6. M and signal isolation
